The idea

No mirrors. No machines telling you you’re done. Just iron, coaching, and consistency.

KILO is a strength club, not a gym. Every session is coached, every lift is programmed, and every member — from first-timer to national qualifier — trains the same waves on the same platforms.

We cap every class, we track every number, and we celebrate ugly, honest PBs louder than pretty warm‑ups. Six weeks in, you’ll see why the board matters.
